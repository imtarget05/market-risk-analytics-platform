# ADR-0001: Risk Calculation Methodology

## Context

The Market Risk Analytics Platform needs to calculate standard market risk metrics for portfolios:

1. Value at Risk (VaR) at configurable confidence levels
2. Expected Shortfall (Conditional VaR)
3. Stress testing under market shock scenarios

## Decision

### VaR Methods

Three VaR methodologies are implemented:

1. **Historical Simulation**: Uses empirical distribution of historical returns. Non-parametric, captures fat tails, but assumes past is representative of future.

2. **Parametric (Variance-Covariance)**: Assumes returns are normally distributed. Uses mean and standard deviation with z-score. Simple but assumes normality (underestimates tail risk).

3. **Monte Carlo**: Simulates random price paths using normal distribution with historical parameters. Flexible, can model non-linear instruments, but computationally intensive.

### Expected Shortfall

Expected Shortfall (Conditional VaR) is the average of returns in the tail beyond the VaR threshold. Coherent risk measure (unlike VaR which is not sub-additive).

### Stress Testing

Applies a percentage shock to all positions and measures P&L impact. Simple but effective for scenario analysis.

### Portfolio Return Calculation

Portfolio returns are calculated as weighted average of individual asset returns, weighted by position quantity. This is a simplified approach suitable for MVP.

### Data Model

- Portfolio: container for positions
- Position: symbol, quantity, average cost
- PriceData: time-series price observations
- RiskCalculation: stored results of risk calculations

## Alternatives Considered

- Exponential Weighted Moving Average (EWMA) for volatility: Rejected for MVP; parametric VaR uses simple standard deviation.
- Copula-based dependence modeling: Rejected; single-factor portfolio return approximation is sufficient for MVP.
